About this dashboard

A free, open-source view of EQTY's on-chain activity on Base, built for the EQTY DAO community. Data is pulled directly from the Base blockchain via the Coinbase CDP SQL API and refreshed once a day (around 05:20 UTC).

  • Holders – how many wallets hold EQTY, how that changed over time, which wallets started or stopped holding, and how active they are.
  • Activity – daily and monthly transfer counts and the amount of EQTY moved.
  • Distribution – holders by balance size and how concentrated the supply is across the 50 largest wallets.
  • Market – price (USD/EUR), buy/sell volume on Uniswap and Gate.io, trade sizes, the latest trades and a trader leaderboard.
  • Whale tracker – large wallet-to-wallet transfers and the most active large senders.

Wallet labels. Some wallets carry a label so you can see who is who. Labels without a mark are verified (detected from the contract itself, or a well-known address). Labels marked ≈ are an estimate based on on-chain behaviour – useful context, but not yet confirmed. Community input is very welcome: suggest or correct a label via GitHub.

Method notes. A holder is a wallet (address) with more than 0.0001 EQTY – not a person. One exchange wallet can hold tokens for many customers, and one person can use several wallets, so holder counts, "new" and "exited" holders and activity groups describe wallets, not people. The Uniswap v4 PoolManager and other contracts are counted as holders but labelled; the top-50 share is also shown without exchanges, the DAO and contracts. "30 days" always means the 30 most recent calendar days in UTC, including today so far (90 days likewise). Suggestions and address labels are welcome via GitHub.

Supply & valuation. Total supply is the on-chain totalSupply of the EQTY contract (minted minus burned). The contract has a 500M cap, but only the bridge wallet could mint, and only until its mintDeadline of 1 Nov 2025. Minting stopped at 291.04M, so that is also the maximum supply: it can never grow and only shrinks through burns (contract source, Hacken audit). Fully diluted value = price × total supply. Circulating supply = total supply minus the DAO treasury Safe; the DAO's pool liquidity is counted as circulating because it can be traded. Market cap = price × circulating supply.

Gate.io. Most EQTY trading happens on Gate.io (EQTY/USDT). Those trades come from Gate's public API and are anonymous: no wallets or labels, and the exchange's market-making bots are included. Buy or sell is the side of the person who took the order, the same as for Uniswap swaps. Gate only shares individual trades for about 30 days, so the buy/sell split starts from the day we began storing them; total daily volume goes back to the listing (Nov 2025). Other exchanges (e.g. CoinSpot) are not included.

Top 50 holders

Who holds the top-50 share, by type of wallet. Exchange wallets hold tokens on behalf of many customers. Whale tracker

Large wallet-to-wallet transfers (swaps, liquidity moves, mints and burns excluded). A transfer counts as large when it is at least 0.25% of the current total supply or worth $10k+ at that day's price. Supply has been stable since the last mint in Oct 2025, so earlier percentages are slightly understated. Large inflows to exchanges can precede selling; transfers between known wallets are often internal moves.

DAO liquidity (Uniswap)

The pool's liquidity is a single full-range position owned by the EQTY DAO (seeded at launch, never withdrawn). It exists so EQTY can be traded on-chain — it is liquidity, not a yield investment.

Position value vs. just holding

What the position is worth over time, versus simply holding the deposited tokens (HODL). The green line adds estimated fees.

Estimated fees (cumulative)
