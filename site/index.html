About this dashboard

A free, open-source view of EQTY's on-chain activity on Base, built for the EQTY DAO community. Data is pulled directly from the Base blockchain via the Coinbase CDP SQL API and refreshed once a day (around 05:20 UTC).

  • Holders – how many wallets hold EQTY, how that changed over time, who joined or left, and how active holders are.
  • Activity – daily and monthly transfer counts and the amount of EQTY moved.
  • Distribution – how concentrated the supply is across the 50 largest wallets.
  • Coming next: price (USD/EUR), Uniswap buy/sell volume and trade sizes, and large wallet transfers.

Method notes. A holder is a wallet with more than 0.0001 EQTY. Supply = minted − burned. The Uniswap v4 PoolManager and other contracts are counted as holders but labelled. Spam pools with 88–99% fees are ignored. Suggestions and address labels are welcome via GitHub.
