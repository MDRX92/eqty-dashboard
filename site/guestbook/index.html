Test version – not live on Base mainnet.

This guestbook runs on Base Sepolia, Base's test network. The test tokens (tEQTY) and test ETH have no value, and no real EQTY is used or burned.

It is a community proposal, not an official EQTY DAO product. It will only go live on Base mainnet if the EQTY DAO reviews it and decides to proceed. Until then there is nothing to buy, nothing to claim and no real tokens are involved. Anyone asking you to use real EQTY here is not us.

Reviewing for the DAO? See For reviewers below. Just trying it? Get free Base Sepolia test ETH from a faucet, connect, then use “Get 1,000 test tokens”.

What is this?

A public guestbook for the EQTY community, written on the Base blockchain. Leave a short message, and to post it you burn a little EQTY. Your words stay on-chain for good, next to everyone else who stopped by.

  • Put your EQTY to use. Do something with the token besides holding or trading it. Each message is a small, real on-chain use of EQTY.
  • Show your support. Say hi, cheer on the EQTY DAO, share why you hold EQTY or what you hope for from Ownables. A visible sign that the community is alive.
  • Make it permanent. Your message is stored on Base with a timestamp and your wallet address. Nobody can edit or delete it, not even us. Proof that you were here.
  • Every message burns EQTY. The fee is destroyed, not paid to anyone: it permanently leaves the total supply, and the running total is shown in the counter below.
  • Be early. Messages are numbered from #0. The first ones will always be the first.
  • A taste of Ownables. Paying EQTY to record something on-chain is the same idea behind Ownables' anchoring. This is a simple way to try it.
  • Cheap and open. Besides the fee, a post costs less than a cent in network fees. The code is open source and the contract is verified, so anyone can check how it works.

Ideas: a greeting from your city · why you hold EQTY · a wish for the DAO · a milestone you want to remember · a shout-out to the builders.
Not financial advice: burning reduces supply, but that doesn't promise anything about the price. Post because it's fun, not as an investment.

    Is this safe? What exactly do I sign?
    • You confirm at most two transactions, both shown in your wallet:
      1. Approve – lets the guestbook burn exactly the fee (–) from your wallet. Not more, and it's used up by your post.
      2. Post – stores your message and burns that fee. Smart wallets that support batching do both in one confirmation.
    • You never send ETH or tokens to anyone, and this page never asks you to sign a message or connect to anything else. If your wallet shows something different (another amount, an unlimited approval, a different contract), reject it.
    • The guestbook contract has no owner and no admin functions: the fee and token are fixed forever, it can't be upgraded and it never holds tokens. Source: EQTYGuestbook.sol.

    Moderation

    Messages live on the blockchain and can't be deleted. This page can hide a message: it then shows "hidden" with the reason instead of the text. The list of hidden messages is public: hidden.json. We only hide scams, abuse, hate and personal data – never criticism. See something that should be hidden? Report it on GitHub with the message number.

    For reviewers (EQTY DAO)

    Everything needed to review this proposal. Summary and checklist: REVIEW.md.

    What it is

    • A guestbook where a message (max 140 characters) costs a fixed amount of EQTY, which is burned (total supply goes down; nobody receives it).
    • Same idea as Ownables' Anchor contract: pay EQTY to put something on-chain. Could be launched on its own or alongside Ownables.
    • Proposed fee: 100 EQTY per message. To be decided by the DAO.

    Contracts – Base Sepolia (testnet), source verified on Basescan

    • EQTYGuestbook: 0x0165…E2b5 (token = TestEQTY, fee = 100 tokens)
    • TestEQTY (test token with faucet, testnet only): 0x6E93…e7b7
    • Source: EQTYGuestbook.sol (~90 lines) · TestEQTY.sol · compiler 0.8.28, optimizer 200, EVM cancun.
    • Mainnet: not deployed. The mainnet version would use the real EQTY token 0xc71f…7d29, which supports burnFrom.

    Security design

    • No owner, no admin functions, not upgradeable: the fee and token are fixed at deployment and nobody can change them.
    • Never holds tokens or ETH: all functions are non-payable; the fee is burned straight from the poster's wallet.
    • The website asks for an approval of exactly the fee (never unlimited), then the post. No signature requests. Smart wallets can do both in one confirmation.
    • Rules enforced on-chain: no links (http, www., ://), no control characters, max 560 bytes. The website also blocks domain names and hidden characters, and shows text as plain text only.
    • Moderation is transparent: messages can't be deleted on-chain; the page hides abuse via a public hide list with reasons.

    Open points for the DAO

    • • Go / no-go, and timing: soft launch now, or together with the Ownables launch.
    • • Fee amount (fixed forever once deployed; a new contract is needed to change it).
    • • Who may hide messages (moderation).
    • • Not professionally audited. A short review by a Solidity developer is recommended before mainnet.
    • • MetaMask may show a phishing warning when connecting to this new page (an automated check on new sites). This is a false positive that can be reported to MetaMask support; an announcement or link from official EQTY channels would also help.
    • • Hosting: currently on GitHub Pages under a community account; could move to a DAO-controlled domain or repository.
