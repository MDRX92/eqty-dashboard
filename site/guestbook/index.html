    Is this safe? What exactly do I sign?
    • You confirm at most two transactions, both shown in your wallet:
      1. Approve – lets the guestbook burn exactly the fee (–) from your wallet. Not more, and it's used up by your post.
      2. Post – stores your message and burns that fee. Smart wallets that support batching do both in one confirmation.
    • You never send ETH or tokens to anyone, and this page never asks you to sign a message or connect to anything else. If your wallet shows something different (another amount, an unlimited approval, a different contract), reject it.
    • The guestbook contract has no owner and no admin functions: the fee and token are fixed forever, it can't be upgraded and it never holds tokens. Source: EQTYGuestbook.sol.
    • Contract: – · Token: –
    • You pay a small network fee in ETH on Base (usually less than one cent).
    Moderation

    Messages live on the blockchain and can't be deleted. This page can hide a message: it then shows "hidden" with the reason instead of the text. The list of hidden messages is public: hidden.json. We only hide scams, abuse, hate and personal data – never criticism. See something that should be hidden? Report it on GitHub with the message number.
